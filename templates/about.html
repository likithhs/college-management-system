A Quarter Century of Educational Excellence

Seshadripuram College was established in 1998 under the aegis of the Seshadripuram Educational Trust (SET), which has been a pioneering landmark in higher education across Karnataka for over eight decades.

Affiliated to Bengaluru City University and accredited with the highest NAAC A++ Grade, the college offers premier undergraduate and postgraduate programs in Computer Applications, Business Administration, Commerce, Arts, and Science.
